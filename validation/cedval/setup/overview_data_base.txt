Data base:

Complexity 0: flow within an "empty" boundary-layer (only roughness elements on test section)
Complexity 1: flow around an isolated obstacle
Complexity 2: flow within regular arrays of obstacles (highly idealized urban geometry)
Complexity 3: flow within a realistic but still idealized urban geometry
Complexity 4: flow within a realistic urban geometry

These folders contain time series as well as the documentations of the experiments 
including pictures and drawings.


*****

Measurement facilities:

The experiments were done in two boundary layer wind tunnels in the EWTL of the University of Hamburg.

Wind tunnel "WOTAN":
- total length of 25 m, width of 4 m, height from 2.75 to 3.25 m
- the test section is 18 m long and has two turn tables to model different approach flow directions
- a adjustable ceiling allows to degree the longitudinal pressure gradient in the test section
- measurements at wind speeds up to 15 m/s are possible
- the instruments can be moved with a 3D probe positioning system (accuracy of 0.1 mm at all axes)

Wind tunnel "BLASIUS":
- total length of 16 m, width of 1.5 m, height of about 1 m
- the test section is 4 m long and has one turn table
- a adjustable ceiling allows to degree the longitudinal pressure gradient in the test section
- measurements at wind speeds up to 10 m/s are possible
- the instruments can be moved with a 3D probe positioning system (accuracy of 0.1 mm at all axes)


Instruments:

- Prandtl tube to measure the reference wind speed
- 2D Laser-Doppler-Anemometry (LDA) for highly time resolved flow measurements (around 500-600 Hz)
  with a focal length between 50 mm and 800 mm depending to the measurement setup
- two 2D LDA systems allow synchronised correlation measurements
- 3D Particle-Image-Velocimetry system (PIV) for highly spatial resolved flow measurements,
  temporal resolution only 15 Hz
- independent reference instruments to calibrate the used measurement instruments


*****

Data processing:

A software package guarantees a fast automatic data processing for the huge amount of measured data.

Scaling to get dimensionless values:

- reference length of 1 m in model scale
- reference height of 100 m in full scale
- reference wind speed is the wind speed at the reference height


All processed data files include a detailed header about:

- scale
- reference height
- reference length
- reference wind speed
- wind tunnel
- coordinates of the location of the measured time series in model scale as well as in full scale
